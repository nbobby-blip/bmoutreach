Supplies across the Gulf Stream.

We are two Florida healthcare workers who carry medical supplies by boat to small clinics in the northern Bahamas. The clinic tells us what it needs. We bring it.

WHY WE STARTED

Small clinic. Long wait for a shipment.

Some island clinics care for a whole community with a small staff, a long way from the nearest hospital.

We both work in patient care in Tallahassee, so we know what it means when the thing you need is not on the shelf. On our first trip we met the team at the Little Grand Cay clinic and left them a load of medical and office supplies. That visit turned into this organization.

We keep it simple. The clinic tells us what it needs. We find it. We bring it over by boat and hand it off in person. The clinic signs for every delivery, so there is always a record of where things went.
